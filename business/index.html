How It Works

One Agent Per Teammate, on Their Own Computer

Archie is our desktop app for building personal AI agents. In a business, that means each person gets an agent shaped to their role, not one shared bot in somebody else’s cloud.

Shaped to Each Role

The person on support gets an agent that triages the inbox and drafts replies. The owner gets one that chases follow-ups and preps the weekly numbers. Same app, different agents: each running on its owner’s computer.

We Never Hold Your Conversations

Customer lists, invoices, inboxes: your business data stays on your computers, in your custody. Your agents talk to the AI company directly on your own accounts, so nothing your team works on passes through us. (The free credits a new install starts with are the one exception: they run on an AI account of ours, and stop being used the moment a teammate connects yours.) Our servers hold each teammate’s email, whether they have a current plan, and which paid add-ons were bought. Two more only where they apply: which version of Archie each computer is running, and, for anyone who turns on phone access, the messages between their computer and their phone, sealed with a key we never receive. No customer data, no documents, no conversations.

It Can’t Email Your Customers or Spend Your Money

Archie can draft and send email and change a connected calendar, but only after you approve that exact action: it proposes, you approve, then it acts. It has no ability to spend money or make a purchase, and that doesn’t exist in the app. An agent speaks only in the chats you connect it to, to teammates you’ve approved. The honest, current list is on our Trust page.

And if Archie can’t do it yet, whether that’s a tool we don’t connect to or a way of working that’s particular to your shop, tell us and we’ll build the add-on. It becomes a marketplace listing too, so the one we build for you helps the next business as well.

Guided Setup

Rather Have Us Walk Your Team Through It?

You can set all of this up yourself, and Archie is built for that. But plenty of owners would rather have someone alongside them the first time. We get on a call with you and your team, build the agents together on your computers, connect accounts with your approval, test everything, and fine-tune after launch. The full process is on How It Works.

No two businesses need the same thing. One owner wants a single agent for replies and scheduling; another has five teammates, a dozen tools, and ten years of process. So we price by the hour rather than by the job: a session is $100 and runs an hour. A bigger setup takes more sessions, never a higher rate, and we tell you how many on the free discovery call.

Archie is $149 a year or $19 a month per person when it ships, separate from guided setup. The AI thinking runs on each person’s own pay-as-you-go account with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq or xAI, typically a few dollars a month each and billed straight to them. We’ll help every teammate get set up.
