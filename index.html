The Short Version

Why Archie, in Three Words

Proactive.

Your morning laid out before you ask. The follow-up flagged before you remembered it. The reply drafted, waiting for one tap. You don’t open Archie. Archie messages you.

Effortless.

No terminal. No code. If you can send a message, you can run an agent. And when you want it to do something new, you just tell it.

Yours.

It runs on your computer, under your accounts. Once your own AI key is connected, your conversations go straight to the AI company and never pass through our servers. You pick an AI provider and how hard it thinks: fast and cheap, or deep and thorough.

You’ve probably seen AI that answers questions. You open it, type, get an answer, close it. By tomorrow it has forgotten you.

Archie works whether you’re there or not. It lives on your computer, keeps an eye on your inbox and calendar, and knocks on your door when something needs attention.

Your Week

Why an AI Agent Is Right for You

If your week fills up with scheduling, catching up on meetings, and remembering who’s waiting on what, that’s exactly the work an agent is built to take on.

Most people don’t go looking for an AI agent. They just have a week that looks like this:

Monday, 9 a.m.

Half an hour disappears reconstructing last week before the new one starts. Ask your agent to catch you up on your meetings and it reads back the transcripts while you pour your coffee.

The reply you keep meaning to send

That email has sat in the back of your head for three days. Your agent writes the draft and drops it in your chat with a Send button, so you tap once, when you’re ready.

The 3 o’clock that has to move

Instead of playing calendar tetris, tell your agent to move it. It proposes the exact change and makes it after you approve.

None of it is an emergency. That’s the point: it’s the small, steady tax on your attention, and an agent is what quietly pays it down. A part-time assistant can run thousands a year and clocks out at five; your agent is there from six in the morning to eleven at night, as long as your computer’s on.

The Honest Week

Skill

Adds up next week’s meetings against the hours actually in it, then tells you plainly what will not fit and what to move, with the changes staged for your approval.

Waiting On

Skill

Reads your sent mail, spots the asks that never got an answer, and tells you who has gone quiet and for how long, with a follow-up already drafted.

The Handover

Skill

Going away? It writes the whole “how this house works” note for whoever is covering: the pets, the plants, the bins, the bills that will charge while you’re gone.

Morning Brief

Routine

Your whole morning in one message: today’s calendar, what’s due, birthdays coming, bills, packages, plus your news and teams. One glance instead of six pings.

Deep Research

Skill

Ask a real question and get a proper written answer: many searches across different angles, sources it actually opened and read, and a plain verdict at the end.

Plain and Patient

Personality

For an agent a grown child sets up for a parent. One idea per sentence, any hard word explained the moment it appears, and the time or the dose repeated at the end.

Every one installs in a click, and each is a starting point rather than a fixed script: tell your agent how you want it to work and it adapts.
